About Me

I'm a Senior Software Engineer at Arrcus Inc., where I work on building distributed infrastructure, system orchestration, and scalable platform features for high-performance network operating systems. I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Colorado Boulder.

At Arrcus, I've led key system initiatives including migrating our OS from a custom orchestrator to systemd, implementing one-step upgrade workflows, and bringing up Broadcom's Tomahawk 5 platform for AI/ML workloads. I also have strong research interests in computer vision and assistive robotics.
